# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The rules page "Näin pelaat": the goal, a turn and the placement rules in short sections, with small
board pictures that show the start corner and the allowed and forbidden contacts.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Rules page with pictures

"Näin pelaat" SHALL explain the goal, a turn, the placement rules and the scoring in short sections
in the game's own words, and SHALL show small board pictures of the first piece on the start corner,
a piece touching the own colour at a corner (allowed) and a piece touching the own colour along an
edge (not allowed), each marked as allowed or not in text as well as colour.

#### Scenario: Pictures

- **WHEN** a player opens "Näin pelaat"
- **THEN** the start corner, corner contact and edge contact pictures are shown with their captions
