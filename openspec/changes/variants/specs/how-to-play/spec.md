## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Variants on the rules page

"Näin pelaat" SHALL have a short section on the variants: Perus, Duo (the small board and its start
squares), Tuplaväri (two colours each, scores added up) and Kolmikko (the shared colour played in
turn, not counted).

#### Scenario: Variants section

- **WHEN** a player opens "Näin pelaat"
- **THEN** a section names the four variants with one or two lines on each
